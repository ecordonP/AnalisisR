# Identidad visual de AnalizaR Desktop

Imágenes creadas con la herramienta integrada de generación de imágenes. El símbolo
combina conversación (burbuja), datos (barras) y análisis (línea con puntos). Los
colores azul y verde siguen los campos de respuesta y consulta de la aplicación.

- `analizar-logo.png`: logotipo horizontal con fondo transparente, para la guía.
- `analizar-icon.png`: símbolo sin texto y fondo transparente.
- `analizar.ico`: el mismo símbolo empaquetado para Windows en tamaños
  16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 128 y 256 píxeles. La conversión no cambia el diseño.

## Prompt del logotipo

Use case: logo-brand. Create one polished original horizontal logo for the Spanish
desktop application 'AnalizaR Desktop', a conversational statistical analysis tool that
runs R locally and presents statistical tables. Transparent background, crisp flat
vector-like artwork, no mockup. At the left a compact distinctive symbol combining
a rounded speech bubble and a simple statistical chart: three vertical rounded
bars of different heights and a short rising connected line, integrated into ONE
coherent mark, not multiple separate icons. Deep navy #15345b with royal blue
#1d4ed8 and a small restrained green #22a66a accent, matching a blue-and-green
statistical research application. At the right exact text 'AnalizaR Desktop' in clean
readable professional semibold sans-serif, navy, with the final uppercase R
accented blue. Generous clean spacing, horizontal composition, symbol and lettering
aligned. No tagline, no additional letters, no robot, no brain, no generic sparkle,
no 3D, no shadows, no gradients. Logo must remain legible on light gray or white and
usable in a local HTML help guide. Produce the finished transparent PNG logo only.

## Prompt del icono

Referencia de imagen: el logotipo generado en el paso anterior.

Use case: logo-brand. Asset: the square desktop APPLICATION ICON corresponding to
the attached AnalizaR Desktop logo. Use the input logo only as a visual identity reference.
Produce ONLY a large single isolated symbol, no wordmark and absolutely no text or
letters. Preserve the identity: a dark navy rounded speech bubble containing three
rising histogram bars (blue, green, blue) and a rising green three-point statistical
line. Redraw as very clean flat vector-like solid color geometry with smooth
antialiased edges. IMPORTANT: remove all mottled edges, colored speckles, texture,
roughness, gradients, shadows and glow. Three or four solid colors only: navy
#15345b, royal blue #1d4ed8, green #22a66a. Strong bold simple shapes readable at
16x16 pixels. Square canvas, centered mark filling about 86 percent of canvas with
modest transparent padding, true transparent background. Do not include the word
AnalizaR Desktop, no frame around the canvas, no mockup. Finished single transparent PNG icon.
